Axe-Throw — how to play

  1. Pull back on the field — like drawing a sling. The dotted arc previews your throw, the ring on the ground marks where it lands, and a gold ring marks a target you're lined up to hit.
  2. Release to throw. Pull farther for more power; the angle of your pull sets the arc.
  3. Hit targets before they cross the range. You have 60 seconds — the last 10 get frantic.

Sneaker Catch — the rules

Slide the shoebox under falling pairs. Regular sneakers pay 20, the rare golden pair pays 60, and a smelly sock costs you 15 and your streak.

Letting a good pair splat on the pavement breaks your streak too — positioning beats panic.

Treat Catch — the rules

Guide the dog under falling snacks. Biscuits pay 10, steak bites pay 30, and chocolate costs 20 — it's genuinely bad for dogs, in the game and out of it.

Both catch games run 45 seconds, and every 3 catches in a row raises your streak multiplier, up to ×4.

Throwing tips

Lead your targets — they keep marching while your axe is in the air, so aim a little ahead of a fast shield.

A short, high lob drops onto close targets. A long flat pull is for the far, fast ones crossing high.

Don't chase a miss. A broken streak costs more than one skipped shield.
